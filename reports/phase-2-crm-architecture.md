## 1. Executive Summary

This document specifies the hardened operational database and CRM architecture for the **Managed Development Partner** business. The system serves as the unified, auditable source of truth across the complete wholesale partnership lifecycle:

$$\text{Discovery} \longrightarrow \text{Research} \longrightarrow \text{Qualification} \longrightarrow \text{Outreach} \longrightarrow \text{Conversation} \longrightarrow \text{Negotiation} \longrightarrow \text{Partnership} \longrightarrow \text{Project Delivery} \longrightarrow \text{Revenue Tracking}$$

The architecture is engineered natively for **Supabase (PostgreSQL 15+)** to support upcoming multi-agent orchestration via **Hermes Agent** while enforcing defensive database-level security and referential integrity.

### Final Hardening Highlights & Architectural Integrity
1. **Migration Separation (MVP Core vs. Delivery Expansion):** To eliminate premature MVP complexity while preserving clean extensibility, the architecture is divided into two distinct migrations:
   - **MVP Core ([`crm/schema.sql`](file:///c:/Users/puria/Desktop/code-workshop/projects/development-partnership/crm/schema.sql) — 14 Tables):** Covers partner discovery, deduplication, contacts, permanent suppression, deep research, 7-dimension qualification, human-gated outreach, multi-channel conversations, customer activities, task queues, and immutable security audit logs.
   - **Delivery & Finance Expansion ([`crm/delivery-governance-expansion.sql`](file:///c:/Users/puria/Desktop/code-workshop/projects/development-partnership/crm/delivery-governance-expansion.sql) — 4 Tables):** Covers master wholesale agreements, client project delivery gates, Technical Lead feasibility evaluation (`is_technical_lead()`), financial completion confirmation (`final_payment_confirmed`), business-day milestone inspections, and change request governance.
2. **Strict Agent Identity & Subagent Privilege Separation:** Eliminates negative-matching security vulnerabilities. Evaluates explicit JWT claims via `is_agent()` and `is_agent_type()`. Unknown roles receive **zero operational access**. Subagents operate under least privilege:
   - `researcher` subagents can only insert research dossiers and findings.
   - `qualifier` subagents can only insert draft scorecards (`review_status = 'draft'`).
   - `drafter` subagents can only insert draft outreach messages (`approval_status = 'draft'`).
   - Agents can only view and update tasks specifically assigned to their verified `current_agent_identity()`.
3. **Airtight Human Approval & Invalidation Gates:**
   - Approvals strictly require a non-null authenticated `auth.uid()`.
   - The approver identity must match the caller (`approved_by_human_id = auth.uid()`).
   - Silent admin identity mismatches are eliminated; administrative overrides are modeled as explicit, auditable actions (`is_admin_override = TRUE`, `override_reason`, `override_by_human_id = auth.uid()`).
   - Approval timestamps are generated by trusted database logic (`clock_timestamp()`).
   - Material edits to approved outreach content or approved lead qualification scores automatically invalidate the approval, resetting the record to `draft`.
4. **Cross-Entity Referential Integrity via Composite Foreign Keys:**
   - Database-level composite foreign keys guarantee that child entities belong to the exact same parent company:
     - `outreach_messages (contact_id, company_id) REFERENCES contacts (id, company_id)`
     - `activities (contact_id, company_id) REFERENCES contacts (id, company_id)`
     - `activities (conversation_id, company_id) REFERENCES conversations (id, company_id)`
     - `research_findings (report_id, company_id) REFERENCES research_reports (id, company_id)`
     - `projects (partnership_id, company_id) REFERENCES partnerships (id, company_id)`
5. **Permanent Multi-Channel Suppression Synchronization:**
   - An independent, permanent [`suppression_registry`](file:///c:/Users/puria/Desktop/code-workshop/projects/development-partnership/crm/schema.sql#L254-L272) table stores lowercased, normalized identifiers across email, domain, LinkedIn, and phone.
   - Triggers automatically sync new identifiers when contacts or companies opt out or change their contact channels.
   - Pre-dispatch triggers verify recipient email, domain, and LinkedIn handle against `suppression_registry` immediately before transmission.
   - Direct deletion from `suppression_registry` is blocked by a trigger exception.
6. **Evidence-Enforced 7-Dimension Lead Qualification:**
   - Preserves the approved 100-point rubric across seven dimensions.
   - Database triggers enforce that any dimension with a score $> 0$ must have documented supporting evidence in `dimension_evidence JSONB`.
   - Database triggers enforce deterministic qualification tier assignments based strictly on approved score thresholds (Tier 1: 80–100, Tier 2: 60–79, Tier 3: 40–59, Disqualified: < 40 or disqualification triggers).
7. **Trusted Database-Side Audit Logging:**
   - Trigger `fn_audit_log_capture()` automatically logs state transitions, approval grants, and revocations into [`audit_logs`](file:///c:/Users/puria/Desktop/code-workshop/projects/development-partnership/crm/schema.sql#L850-L868).
   - An immutability trigger blocks all manual `UPDATE` and `DELETE` operations on `audit_logs`.
8. **Timezone-Aware Business-Day Calculations:**
   - The function `add_business_days(start_ts, num_days, tz)` is declared `STABLE` and calculates milestone deadlines by evaluating working days (Mon–Fri) in the partner's local timezone, skipping Saturdays and Sundays.

## 2. Architecture Principles

1. **Normalized Core Entities with Pragmatic Semi-Structured Data:** Core entities are fully normalized (3NF) with composite keys. `JSONB` is reserved strictly for discovery details, evidence justifications, and immutable audit snapshots.
2. **UUID Primary Keys Everywhere:** All entities utilize `UUIDv4` primary keys (`gen_random_uuid()`) to eliminate enumeration vulnerabilities.
3. **Auditability and Strict Attribution:** Every transactional table maintains trigger-updated timestamps. High-stakes state transitions record actor classification (`human`, `agent`, `system`) and verifiable actor identities.
4. **Defensive Database Constraints:** Business rules and commercial boundaries are implemented at the schema layer (composite FKs, CHECK constraints, defensive triggers).
5. **Zero-Assumption Lead Scoring:** Lead qualification enforces a 100-point rubric. Unknown information receives 0 points by default. Speculative positive scoring is prohibited.
6. **Partner Client Protection as Data Invariant:** The partner owns 100% of the relationship with the end client. End-client records are never stored as independent marketable prospects. Behind-the-scenes white-label is the default delivery mode.
7. **Permanent Suppression & Compliance:** Opt-outs reside in an immutable registry that survives parent entity deletions.
8. **Pragmatic MVP Scoping:** Explicit separation between MVP Core (14 tables) and Later Delivery & Finance (4 tables).

## 3. Entity Definitions & Schema Architecture

The platform architecture models eighteen tables divided across two migration phases:

### 3.1. MVP Core Tables ([`crm/schema.sql`](file:///c:/Users/puria/Desktop/code-workshop/projects/development-partnership/crm/schema.sql))
1. **`suppression_registry`:** Independent, permanent ledger storing suppressed emails, domains, LinkedIn handles, and phone numbers. Survives all deletions.
2. **`companies`:** Central registry of partner agencies and studios. Captures market segment, verified tech stack, verified headcount, and lifecycle stage.
3. **`company_domains`:** Multi-domain mapping for duplicate detection. Automatically synchronized with `companies.primary_domain`.
4. **`contacts`:** Professional contacts and decision-makers. Includes composite unique key `(id, company_id)` and channel opt-out flags.
5. **`research_reports`:** Dossiers compiled by Hermes subagents with explicit TBD gap tracking. Composite unique key `(id, company_id)`.
6. **`research_findings`:** Atomic evidence statements. Composite FK `(report_id, company_id) REFERENCES research_reports (id, company_id)`.
7. **`lead_qualifications`:** 7-dimension scorecards with database-validated evidence and human approval gates.
8. **`outreach_campaigns`:** Segmented prospecting initiatives (Digital Agencies, Webflow Studios, Framer Studios).
9. **`outreach_messages`:** Draft correspondence with composite FK `(contact_id, company_id)`, database-generated approval timestamps, admin overrides, and rev-share prohibition.
10. **`conversations`:** Active dialogue threads and objection logging. Composite unique key `(id, company_id)`.
11. **`conversation_messages`:** Chronological message log across email, LinkedIn, and calls.
12. **`activities`:** Unified commercial engagement timeline (calls, meetings, notes, status changes) with composite FKs.
13. **`tasks`:** Operational task queue with row-level ownership restricted by agent identity.
14. **`audit_logs`:** Immutable, append-only security ledger populated by database triggers.

### 3.2. Delivery & Finance Expansion Tables ([`crm/delivery-governance-expansion.sql`](file:///c:/Users/puria/Desktop/code-workshop/projects/development-partnership/crm/delivery-governance-expansion.sql))
15. **`partnerships`:** Master wholesale agreements, non-circumvention invariants, and white-label default terms. Composite unique key `(id, company_id)`.
16. **`projects`:** Discrete client projects with composite FK `(partnership_id, company_id) REFERENCES partnerships (id, company_id)`, lead feasibility reviews, and human delivery authorization gates.
17. **`project_milestones`:** Deliverable milestones with true business-day inspection windows and 2-revision limits.
18. **`project_change_requests`:** Formal out-of-scope change orders requiring lead engineer review and mutual sign-off.

## 5. Security Architecture & Privilege Matrix

### 5.1. Agent Identity & Role Claim Model
Supabase authentication evaluates custom claims present in `auth.jwt() -> 'app_metadata'`:
- Human Operator: `user_role IN ('operator', 'admin', 'founder')`
- Automated Agent: `user_role = 'agent'` with sub-claim `subagent_type IN ('researcher', 'qualifier', 'drafter')` and `agent_id = '<uuid>'`
- Unknown / Missing Role: Evaluates to `FALSE` for both `is_human_operator()` and `is_agent()`. **Zero operational access granted.**

### 5.3. Trusted Backend & Service-Role Isolation
> [!CAUTION]
> **Service-Role Boundary:** In Supabase, the `service_role` key bypasses Row Level Security by design.
> 
> Under no circumstances may the `service_role` key be provided to Hermes subagents, browser clients, or external automation runners. Hermes subagents authenticate using standard scoped JWT tokens with `user_role = 'agent'`.

## 6. Data Integrity & Approval Enforcement Mechanisms

### 6.1. Outreach Approval & Invalidation Flow
1. **Draft Creation:** Drafter agent inserts draft message with `approval_status = 'draft'`.
2. **Review & Approval:** Human operator calls update setting `approval_status = 'approved'`.
   - Trigger verifies `is_human_operator() = TRUE`.
   - Trigger verifies `approved_by_human_id = auth.uid()` (or explicit auditable admin override).
   - Trigger overwrites `approved_at = clock_timestamp()`.
3. **Automatic Invalidation on Content Modification:** If an approved message has its `body`, `subject`, `channel`, `contact_id`, or `proposed_commercial_model` modified, trigger `fn_enforce_outreach_human_approval` automatically resets `approval_status = 'draft'`, `approved_by_human_id = NULL`, and `approved_at = NULL`.
4. **Pre-Dispatch Suppression Check:** When transitioning to `queued`, `sending`, or `sent`, the trigger checks the contact's email, LinkedIn handle, and company domain against `suppression_registry`. Any match aborts the transaction immediately.

### 6.2. Lead Qualification Evidence & Tier Validation Flow
1. **Draft Assessment:** Qualifier agent inserts scorecard with `review_status = 'draft'`.
2. **Evidence Validation:** Trigger verifies that any dimension with score $> 0$ has structured evidence in `dimension_evidence -> '<dimension>' ->> 'evidence'`. Missing evidence throws an exception.
3. **Tier Agreement:** Trigger validates that `qualification_tier` corresponds exactly to the 7-dimension score sum and disqualification array:
   - Score $\ge 80$ without disqualifiers $\longrightarrow$ `tier_1_priority`
   - Score 60–79 without disqualifiers $\longrightarrow$ `tier_2_prospective`
   - Score 40–59 without disqualifiers $\longrightarrow$ `tier_3_deferred`
   - Score $< 40$ OR disqualification reasons present $\longrightarrow$ `disqualified`
4. **Human Review:** Human operator sets `review_status = 'approved'`. Trigger verifies `reviewed_by_human_id = auth.uid()`.
5. **Score Mutation Invalidation:** Modifying any dimension score or evidence resets `review_status = 'draft'`.

## 7. Business Calendar & Inspection Deadline Calculation

The deterministic function `add_business_days(start_ts TIMESTAMPTZ, num_days INT, tz TEXT DEFAULT 'UTC')`:
- Declared **`STABLE`** (correct volatility for timezone-dependent calculations).
- Evaluates `EXTRACT(DOW FROM curr_ts AT TIME ZONE tz)` to accurately skip Saturdays (DOW 6) and Sundays (DOW 0).
- Automatically looks up the partner company's local operating timezone from `companies.timezone`.
- Guarantees the partner a full 5 working days for milestone review without calendar weekend distortion. Statutory public holidays are explicitly deferred to application logic / Phase 3.
