# Business Overview — Managed Development Partner

## 1. Executive Summary & Operational Stage

The business is structured as a **Managed Development Partner**. Its core purpose is to establish long-term, high-trust wholesale partnerships with digital agencies, independent studios, freelancers, and businesses that already possess established client relationships and recurring software engineering requirements.

### Current Operational Stage: Pre-Operational Foundation
The business is currently in a **pre-operational foundation-building stage**. 
- The planned technical team is **not yet onboarded** and is **not currently available for client delivery**.
- Infrastructure, operating procedures, service definitions, and multi-agent coordination architectures are being established prior to active market engagement.
- All references in this document represent the planned operational framework and strategic priorities; the business is not currently delivering client projects.

---

## 2. Core Value Proposition

- **Managed Wholesale Technical Capacity:** Turnkey software development capacity managed through centralized engineering oversight, enabling partners to expand their delivery capabilities without hiring permanent technical staff.
- **Partner Protection & Relationship Ownership:** The partner retains 100% ownership of their client relationships. We operate on a strict non-circumvention basis, with **behind-the-scenes white-label** as the default delivery model.
- **Disciplined Commercial Sequencing:** A staged commercial structure that allows partners to validate execution quality with low-friction fixed-scope projects before transitioning into recurring monthly capacity reservations.
- **AI-Assisted Efficiency:** Utilizing modern AI-assisted engineering workflows under senior technical supervision, coordinated by a multi-agent business development infrastructure (Hermes Agent).

---

## 3. Commercial Model Hierarchy

The business implements a hybrid commercial model structured in three clear evolutionary phases:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Project-Based Wholesale Development │  ◄── Initial Entry Model (Low Friction, Trust-Building)
└───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                    ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 2. Monthly Reserved Capacity Retainers │  ◄── Core Recurring Revenue Engine (Predictable Scale)
└───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                    ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 3. Strategic Revenue-Sharing Model     │  ◄── Selective Tier (Vetted Partners Only; No Cold Outreach)
└────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Project-Based Wholesale Development (Initial Entry Model):**
   - The primary entry on-ramp designed to establish trust, validate workflow synergy, and prove technical execution with minimal partner risk.
   - Delivered as fixed-scope or milestone-driven engagements.
   - Specific pricing floors and milestone metrics: `TBD`.

2. **Monthly Reserved Development Capacity (Core Recurring Revenue Model):**
   - The primary recurring revenue objective, offered to partners once delivery trust, communication cadence, and consistent project pipeline are proven.
   - Partners reserve dedicated monthly engineering bandwidth for active client rosters.
   - Dedicated capacity units, minimum commitment periods, and rate structures: `TBD`.

3. **Strategic Revenue-Sharing Partnerships (Selective Model):**
   - A selective arrangement reserved exclusively for vetted, high-alignment partners with established commercial track records.
   - **Never offered during cold outreach or standard prospecting.**
   - Every agreement must explicitly define the calculation basis (recognized revenue, allowable deductions, direct costs, collection timing, refunds, and audit rights) to ensure delivery costs and risks are protected.
   - Standard business model strictly excludes equity-sharing arrangements.
   - Commercial percentage ranges: `TBD`.

## 4. Target Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Initial Target Markets (Three Distinct Segments)

1. **Digital Agencies:**
   - Full-service, creative, branding, and marketing agencies managing active client rosters with technical development overflow or lacking in-house engineering departments.
2. **Webflow Studios:**
   - Specialized visual development studios building on Webflow that require custom backend connectivity, database extensions, user authentication, and third-party API integrations beyond visual builder limits.
3. **Framer Studios:**
   - Visual development studios designing in Framer that require custom React component development, bespoke application logic, serverless API handling, and advanced interactive capabilities.

*(Note: Webflow and Framer studios are recognized and treated as distinct market segments due to different underlying technology stacks, developer workflows, and client requirements.)*

### Phase 2: Future Target Markets (Planned Expansion)
- **E-commerce Agencies:** Agencies building custom commerce storefronts and headless platforms.
- **Independent Consultants and Freelancers:** Product and design strategists requiring engineering execution for client roadmaps.
- **Small SaaS Companies:** Early-stage software companies requiring scalable engineering support.
- **Other Businesses with Recurring Software Demand:** Any business with consistent, ongoing software development backlogs.

## 7. Partner and Client Relationship Protection

- **Partner Relationship Exclusivity:** The partner owns the commercial and contractual relationship with the end client.
- **Strict Non-Circumvention:** Direct commercial solicitation of a partner's clients is strictly prohibited.
- **Communication Protocol:** Direct communication between our team and the partner’s end client is prohibited unless explicitly authorized in writing by the partner.
- **Branding Mode:** All work defaults to **behind-the-scenes white-label**. Co-branded delivery is permitted only by prior written consent.
