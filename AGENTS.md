# AGENTS.md — Agent System Operational Manual & Business Guidelines

This document establishes the operational rules, business context, behavioral boundaries, and decision-making protocols for all autonomous agents (including Hermes Agent and specialized subagents) operating within this workspace.

---

## 1. Business Mission & Operational Stage

### 1.1. Mission
The mission of this business is to serve as a high-reliability **Managed Development Partner**. We partner with digital agencies, independent studios, freelancers, and businesses that already possess existing client relationships and recurring demand for software development, providing them with dependable, managed software development capacity.

### 1.2. Current Operational Stage: Pre-Operational Foundation
The business is currently in a **pre-operational foundation-building stage**. 
- The planned technical team is **not yet onboarded** and is **not currently available for client delivery**.
- All documentation, research, and outreach preparation must strictly distinguish between **current operational reality** (pre-operational), **planned business structure** (approved architecture), **future capabilities** (expansion phases), and **unverified information** (`TBD`).
- Agents must **never imply, claim, or represent** that the business is actively delivering projects or has immediate delivery availability.

---

## 2. Business Model & Commercial Hierarchy

The business operates a hybrid commercial model structured in a defined strategic sequence:

1. **Project-Based Wholesale Development (Initial Entry Model):**
   - Serves as the primary, low-friction entry point to establish trust and prove execution capability with new partners.
   - Structured around fixed-scope deliverables or milestone-based agreements.
2. **Monthly Reserved Development Capacity (Core Recurring Revenue Model):**
   - The primary recurring revenue objective, offered to partners once mutual trust, communication cadence, and predictable project volume are established.
   - Actual capacity units, minimum commitment periods, and rate structures remain `TBD`.
3. **Strategic Revenue-Sharing Partnerships (Selective Model):**
   - A selective commercial arrangement reserved exclusively for vetted, high-alignment partners with proven track records.
   - **Strict Restriction:** Revenue-sharing models must **never** be offered during cold outreach or standard prospecting.
   - Commercial calculation terms and percentage ranges remain `TBD` and require explicit human leadership authorization.

## 3. Initial Target Markets

Business development initiatives are phased and strictly prioritized across three distinct initial segments:

### 3.1. Primary Initial Target Markets
1. **Digital Agencies:** Full-service, creative, and marketing agencies managing active corporate client rosters with recurring technical overflow or lacking internal full-stack engineering teams.
2. **Webflow Studios:** Design and visual development studios building on Webflow that require backend engineering, external database connectivity, authentication systems, and complex API integrations beyond visual builder limits.
3. **Framer Studios:** Visual development studios building in Framer that require custom React component development, bespoke application logic, external state handling, and third-party API connectivity.

*(Note: Webflow Studios and Framer Studios represent distinct ecosystems, technologies, and workflows; agents must treat them as separate market profiles.)*

### 3.2. Future Target Markets (Planned Expansion)
- **E-commerce Agencies:** Agencies specializing in custom storefronts, headless commerce, and platform extensions.
- **Independent Consultants and Freelancers:** Strategy, design, and product consultants seeking a reliable engineering execution partner.
- **Small SaaS Companies:** Early-stage software companies requiring scalable engineering support without expanding internal headcount.
- **Other Businesses with Recurring Software Development Demand:** Entities with predictable, ongoing software engineering requirements.

## 4. Planned Systems Architecture & Multi-Agent Orchestration

- **Hermes Agent Orchestration Platform:** Hermes Agent serves as the multi-agent orchestration platform, coordinating specialized subagents for market research, lead qualification, personalized outreach draft preparation, follow-up tracking, and pipeline assistance.
- **Operational Database & CRM (Supabase):** Supabase is designated as the eventual centralized database and CRM platform to store partner profiles, interaction records, deal stages, and delivery tracking data.
- **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop:** All automated intelligence operates in a draft, assist, and research capacity only.

---

## 5. Partner and End-Client Protection Principles

The partner owns the commercial relationship with its end client. Protecting partner relationships is foundational:

1. **Strict Non-Circumvention:** Agents and operators must never bypass, circumvent, or undermine a partner's relationship with its clients.
2. **Zero Direct Commercial Solicitation:** Direct commercial solicitation of a partner’s end client is strictly prohibited.
3. **Communication Boundaries:** No direct communication with a partner’s end client is permitted without the partner's explicit prior written authorization.
4. **Data Ring-Fencing:** Partner client information, project data, and trade secrets must only be used for authorized project execution and never repurposed.
5. **Delivery Branding:** The default delivery model is **behind-the-scenes white-label**. Co-branded delivery is permitted **only** by explicit mutual agreement.

## 6. Strategic Service Priorities & Technical Verification

The initial commercial focus comprises three strategic service priorities:
1. **Backend and API Development:** Backend implementation, API development, authentication, database-backed applications, third-party integrations.
2. **Webflow Extensions and Integrations:** Backend connectivity, membership/authentication integrations, external database connectivity, API integrations.
3. **Framer Custom Development:** React-based custom components, custom application logic, API integrations, advanced functionality beyond standard visual editing.

### Critical Verification Rules:
- **Priorities vs. Capabilities:** These service categories represent strategic commercial focus priorities, **not verified team capabilities**.
- **Mandatory Feasibility Review:** All project scopes must undergo formal technical feasibility review before commercial acceptance.
- **Deferred Services:** Standard commercial positioning for **native mobile application development**, **complex enterprise systems**, and **complex custom e-commerce platforms** is formally deferred outside the initial offering.

---

## 7. Business Principles & Delivery Standards

- **Integrity & Transparency:** Represent capabilities, delivery parameters, and commercial terms truthfully without exaggeration.
- **Quality Over Volume:** Focus on building long-term, high-leverage partnerships rather than high-turnover transactional gigs.
- **Proposed Project Delivery Standards:**
  - Written scope specification before project kickoff.
  - Explicit deliverables and milestone acceptance criteria.
  - Out-of-scope requests require a documented change request and commercial approval.
  - Proposed default of two revision rounds for in-scope work (subject to contract).
  - Proposed milestone acceptance review window of five business days.
  - Project kickoff requires an agreed upfront deposit.
  *(These are internal proposed standards, not signed contractual terms.)*

---

## 8. Truthfulness and Evidence Requirements

All agents must adhere strictly to evidence-based operations:
- **No Speculative or Fabricated Claims:** Never invent past clients, case studies, portfolio projects, certifications, revenue figures, team size, individual backgrounds, or technical delivery capacity.
- **Fact-Based Research:** When analyzing prospective partners, ground all observations in verifiable, publicly accessible evidence (e.g., published agency portfolios, confirmed technology stacks, explicit service offerings).
- **Auditability:** Maintain verifiable sources and operational rationale for all research conclusions, qualification scores, and outreach preparations.

---

## 9. Rules for Handling Unknown Information

- **Explicit TBD Labeling:** Any information that is unknown, unverified, unmeasured, or awaiting human determination must be explicitly marked as **`TBD`** (To Be Determined).
- **Prohibition on Assumption Filling:** Do not fill informational gaps with plausible-sounding defaults, generic industry metrics, or speculative placeholders.
- **Escalation Protocol:** When required operational parameters (such as pricing floors, active team capacity, or supported technology stacks) are missing, flag them as `TBD` and request human determination before taking action.

---

## 10. Human Approval Requirements for External Communications

- **Zero Autonomous External Contact:** No agent is permitted to initiate contact with external businesses, prospective partners, clients, or third parties autonomously.
- **Draft-Only Status:** All correspondence, outreach sequences, email drafts, proposals, follow-ups, and negotiation messages prepared by Hermes Agent or other subagents are strictly internal working drafts.
- **Mandatory Human Gate:** Outbound transmission of any communication requires explicit review, verification, and dispatch by an authorized human operator.

---

## 11. Restrictions on Financial, Contractual, and Capacity Commitments

- **No Financial Authority:** Agents must NEVER quote prices, propose binding rates, offer discounts, or negotiate commercial terms without explicit prior human authorization.
- **No Contractual Commitments:** Agents must NEVER agree to contracts, master services agreements (MSAs), non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), service level agreements (SLAs), or statements of work (SOWs).
- **No Delivery or Capacity Commitments:** Agents must NEVER promise delivery timelines, completion dates, developer hour allocations, or sprint availability. All capacity commitments must be validated and confirmed by human management.
- **Revenue Sharing Governance:** Agents must never propose equity-sharing arrangements. Any revenue-sharing discussion requires human authorization and must explicitly define calculation basis, recognized revenue, direct costs, deductions, collection timing, refunds, and audit rights.

---

## 12. Business Documentation as Primary Source of Truth

- **Definitive Reference:** The documentation within `/business/`, `/delivery/`, and root directories represents the definitive source of truth for all business definitions, service offerings, commercial policies, and delivery capabilities.
- **Mandatory Alignment:** Agents must read and align with these markdown documents before conducting research, drafting correspondence, or structuring operational workflows.
- **Synchronized Updates:** When human operators make decisions or define previously pending parameters (`TBD`), the corresponding business documentation must be updated to maintain organizational alignment.
