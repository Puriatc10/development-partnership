# Commercial Policy & Governance

## 1. Commercial Philosophy & Pre-Operational Context

The business operates as a **Managed Development Partner**, prioritizing sustainable, long-term wholesale partnerships over transactional, one-off project gigs. 

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Pre-Operational Status:** The business is currently in a pre-operational foundation-building stage. The planned technical team is not yet onboarded and not currently available for client delivery. 
> 
> All rate structures, pricing floors, and payment mechanics listed as `TBD` require formal determination by human leadership. Automated agents have zero authority to propose or confirm financial or contractual commitments.

## 2. Commercial Model Hierarchy

Commercial engagements are structured across three sequential tiers designed to systematically build trust and scale predictable revenue:

```
Tier 1: Project-Based Wholesale (Entry Model)
   └── Low-friction, fixed-scope projects to establish trust and validate synergy.
Tier 2: Monthly Reserved Capacity (Recurring Revenue Model)
   └── Dedicated engineering bandwidth allocated for ongoing partner client retainers.
Tier 3: Strategic Revenue Sharing (Selective Tier)
   └── Exclusive partnership model for vetted partners; strictly prohibited in cold outreach.
```

### 2.1. Project-Based Wholesale Development (Entry Model)
- **Role:** The primary entry on-ramp to de-risk collaboration for new partners.
- **Contract Structure:** Fixed-scope or milestone-driven Scope of Work (SOW).
- **Pricing & Scoping Floors:** `TBD` (Subject to project-by-project technical feasibility review).
- **Payment Structure:** Agreed upfront deposit required prior to project kickoff, with remaining payments tied to milestone sign-offs.

### 2.2. Monthly Reserved Development Capacity (Recurring Revenue Model)
- **Role:** The primary recurring revenue engine, offered after execution trust and continuous project volume have been established.
- **Contract Structure:** Recurring monthly reservation allocating dedicated engineering capacity across partner projects.
- **Capacity Units & Retainer Rates:** `TBD` (Capacity metrics, hourly caps, or sprint allocations to be determined).
- **Commitment Terms:** `TBD` (Minimum commitment durations subject to executive approval).

### 2.3. Strategic Revenue-Sharing Partnerships (Selective Model)
- **Role:** Highly selective commercial model reserved for established, deeply vetted partners with demonstrated client pipelines.
- **Outreach Restriction:** **Strictly prohibited as an offering during cold outreach or standard prospecting.**
- **Governance Requirements:**
  - Standard business model strictly excludes equity-sharing arrangements.
  - Every agreement must explicitly define the calculation basis: recognized revenue, allowable deductions, direct development costs, collection timing, refund provisions, scope boundaries, reporting cadence, and audit rights.
  - Commercial terms must guarantee that direct engineering costs and delivery risks are protected against client defaults or scope overruns.
  - Percentage ranges and profit/revenue formulas: `TBD`.

## 3. Proposed Default Project Delivery Standards

To maintain commercial and operational integrity, the following internal proposed standards are established:

> [!NOTE]
> These standards represent **internal proposed defaults**, not executed legal contracts. Specific figures and clauses remain subject to formal human determination and contract negotiation.

1. **Mandatory Written Scope:** All engagements require a signed, written Scope of Work (SOW) defining technical specifications, deliverables, and explicit boundaries before development begins.
2. **Upfront Project Deposit:** Development kickoffs require an agreed upfront payment. (Standard deposit percentages remain `TBD`).
3. **Milestone Acceptance & Inspection Window:**
   - Deliverables are reviewed against written acceptance criteria.
   - Proposed default inspection window: **five business days** per milestone for partner review and written defect reporting. Work is deemed accepted if no deviations are submitted within this window.
4. **Revision Round Baseline:** Proposed default of **two revision rounds** for in-scope deliverables. Additional revisions or modifications exceeding original specifications require formal change requests.
5. **Change Request Governance:** Any alteration to scope, architecture, or deliverables requires a written Change Request detailing timeline and commercial adjustments, subject to mutual human approval.
6. **Warranty & Bug-Fix Period:** `TBD`.

## 4. Partner and End-Client Protection Policy

The partner owns the commercial and strategic relationship with the end client. We enforce strict contractual safeguards:

1. **Strict Non-Circumvention:** We will never solicit, contract with, or divert business from a partner’s end client.
2. **Zero Direct Commercial Solicitation:** Direct commercial solicitation of any partner client is strictly prohibited across all channels.
3. **Communication Boundaries:** Our team will not communicate directly with a partner’s end client without explicit prior written authorization from the partner.
4. **Client Data Confidentiality:** All end-client data, intellectual property, and project assets are strictly confidential and ring-fenced for authorized project execution only.
5. **Branding Mode:**
   - **Default:** Behind-the-scenes white-label execution under the partner's brand.
   - **Exception:** Co-branded delivery is permitted only by explicit, mutual written agreement.

## 6. Governance & Approval Authorities

To safeguard the commercial viability of the business:

### 6.1. Agent Authority Boundaries
- **Zero Pricing Authority:** Autonomous agents (including Hermes Agent and subagents) must NEVER quote prices, propose binding rates, offer discounts, or negotiate commercial terms.
- **Zero Contractual Authority:** Agents must NEVER accept, sign, or deliver binding agreements, MSAs, NDAs, or SOWs.
- **Zero Capacity Guarantees:** Agents must NEVER promise developer start dates, delivery turnaround times, or sprint allocations.

### 6.2. Mandatory Human Approval Gates
- **Outbound Quotations & Proposals:** Every commercial proposal, quote, and rate card must receive explicit written approval from authorized human management before transmission.
- **Contract Execution:** All binding agreements must be executed by authorized human leadership.
- **Capacity Bookings:** Any monthly capacity reservation must be verified against actual engineering capacity by human operations.
