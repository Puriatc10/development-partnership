# Ideal Partner Profile (IPP) — Target Market Specifications

## 1. Objective & Operational Stage

This document establishes the Ideal Partner Profiles (IPP), qualification criteria, and disqualification signals for business development initiatives.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Pre-Operational Status:** The business is currently in a pre-operational foundation-building stage. The planned technical delivery team is not yet onboarded and not currently available for client delivery. 
> 
> All profiling, lead research, and outreach preparation performed by Hermes Agent or human operators must respect pre-operational constraints and prioritize relationship-building over immediate delivery commitments.

## 2. Priority 1: Initial Target Markets (Three Distinct Segments)

The initial target market consists of three clearly separated commercial categories:

### 2.1. Digital Agencies
- **Profile Summary:** Independent, boutique, and mid-sized creative, full-service, or marketing agencies managing a roster of active corporate, B2B, or consumer brand clients.
- **Key Characteristics:**
  - Strong competencies in brand strategy, UI/UX design, marketing, and client account management.
  - Sells comprehensive digital solutions but faces internal engineering bottlenecks or lacks a full-time, in-house full-stack software team.
  - Recurring client inquiries for custom web applications, customer portals, internal management tools, and backend integrations.
- **Core Pain Points:**
  - Losing high-value contracts or turning down client projects due to engineering capacity limitations.
  - Unreliability, uneven code quality, and communication friction from ad-hoc freelance developers.
  - High financial overhead and management risk associated with hiring full-time senior engineers.
- **Key Technical Needs:** Backend implementation, API endpoints, authentication workflows, database architecture, and ongoing technical maintenance.
- **Firmographic Indicators:** Team size: 5–50; Geography: `TBD`; Average client project budget: `TBD`.

### 2.2. Webflow Studios
- **Profile Summary:** Specialized design and visual development studios focused on building premium marketing and corporate websites on the Webflow platform.
- **Key Characteristics:**
  - Elite visual design, typography, interactions, and Webflow CMS build capabilities.
  - Fast-growing studios whose clients increasingly request functional capabilities that surpass native Webflow builder features.
- **Core Pain Points:**
  - Hitting the ceiling of Webflow CMS limits (e.g., collection item caps, multi-reference constraints).
  - Client requests for custom user portals, member authentication, dynamic relational data, and payment gateways that require custom external architecture.
  - Fear of losing client relationships to full-service software agencies when complex functionality is requested.
- **Key Technical Needs:** Webflow API integrations, external database connectivity (e.g., Supabase/PostgreSQL), user authentication integration (e.g., Memberstack/custom auth), and serverless webhook processing.
- **Firmographic Indicators:** Studio size: 2–15; Platform focus: Primary Webflow; Geography: `TBD`.

### 2.3. Framer Studios
- **Profile Summary:** Cutting-edge visual design and product studios building interactive, modern websites and micro-sites using Framer.
- **Key Characteristics:**
  - Highly design-forward, interaction-focused studios with strong Figma-to-Framer workflows.
  - Clients demanding high-performance web experiences with custom dynamic behavior, complex animations, or embedded web application functionality.
- **Core Pain Points:**
  - Needing bespoke React-based components that require specialized JavaScript/TypeScript code.
  - Integrating custom client-side state, external live data feeds, or third-party SaaS APIs that Framer does not support out-of-the-box.
  - Lack of in-house React developers to bridge the gap between visual design and custom software code.
- **Key Technical Needs:** Custom React component authoring, state management logic, serverless API endpoint connectivity, and dynamic interactive extensions.
- **Firmographic Indicators:** Studio size: 2–15; Platform focus: Primary Framer; Geography: `TBD`.

## 4. Comprehensive Partner Qualification Criteria

To qualify as an active prospective partner, an entity must satisfy five foundational criteria:

1. **Demonstrated Recurring Client Demand:**
   - Must possess an active client roster or demonstrable, recurring pipeline of external client projects.
   - Evidence of ongoing client retainers or multi-month project engagements.
2. **Commercial & Contractual Maturity:**
   - Must hold formal, direct contractual agreements with their end clients.
   - Established payment and invoicing processes with verified commercial stability.
3. **Project Scoping Maturity:**
   - Ability to define client requirements clearly, provide structured design assets (e.g., Figma files), and collaborate on written Scopes of Work (SOW).
   - Willingness to operate under formal milestone acceptance criteria and change request procedures.
4. **Partner Technical Literacy:**
   - Appreciation of software development fundamentals, realistic production timelines, and testing cycles.
   - Receptivity to a delivery model structured around an experienced technical lead overseeing junior developers utilizing modern AI-assisted engineering workflows.
5. **Collaborative Wholesale Fit:**
   - Commitment to a true partnership model operating via behind-the-scenes white-label execution.
   - Acceptance of strict partner ownership of the client relationship and mutual non-circumvention.

## 5. Commercial Outreach Alignment Rules

- **Standard Cold Outreach Models:**
  - Outreach must focus exclusively on **Project-Based Wholesale Development** as the low-risk entry vehicle, introducing **Monthly Reserved Capacity** as the natural progression once trust is established.
- **Revenue-Sharing Prohibition in Cold Outreach:**
  - **Strict Rule:** Strategic Revenue-Sharing is **never** offered, proposed, or mentioned during cold outreach or initial discovery calls. It is reserved exclusively for vetted partners with proven project history and shared commercial alignment.

---

## 6. Disqualification Signals

Prospects exhibiting any of the following characteristics must be disqualified:

- **Speculative / Zero-Client Entities:** Individuals or startup founders with no active paying clients seeking speculative development or unpaid MVP builds.
- **Transactional Commodity Shoppers:** Businesses seeking the cheapest ad-hoc developer labor with zero interest in an ongoing partnership or quality standards.
- **Lack of Scoping Maturity:** Prospects unable or unwilling to define project scope in writing or who demand unbounded revisions without change orders.
- **Unethical or High-Risk Practices:** Entities unwilling to agree to mutual non-circumvention or exhibiting questionable business legitimacy.
