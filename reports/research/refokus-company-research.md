## 1. Executive Summary

Refokus is an elite digital design and creative technology studio and certified **Webflow Enterprise Partner**. Founded in early 2021 by Leonardo (Leo) Zakour and Stefanie (Steffi) Zakour, the agency operates a remote-first, globally distributed model with corporate hubs in Hamburg, Germany and New York City, USA. The agency specializes in brand positioning, visual identity, high-end motion graphics, and advanced custom Webflow engineering for high-growth B2B tech startups, venture-backed scale-ups, and global brands.

With over 200 delivered projects, numerous international awards (Awwwards, CSS Design Awards, FWA), and the creation of the widely adopted **Refokus Tools** ecosystem (`tools.refokus.com`), Refokus represents an archetypal primary target for a behind-the-scenes Managed Development Partner. ## 6. Strategic Service Alignment & Collaboration Hypothesis

Our business focuses on three strategic service priorities:
1. **Backend & API Development:** Building database-backed logic, authentication, and external middleware.
2. **Webflow Extensions & Integrations:** Connecting Webflow sites to external databases (PostgreSQL/Supabase), member portals, and third-party APIs.
3. **Framer & Custom React Development:** Bespoke React componentry and state handling.

### Collaboration Fit Analysis:
- **Core Creative vs. Engineering Boundary:** Refokus's core competitive moat is visual design, motion art, and brand narrative. Handling deep backend architectures (multi-tenant databases, transactional workflows, CRM webhooks) can dilute focus and introduce delivery friction.
- **White-Label Managed Capacity:** Providing dependable, behind-the-scenes wholesale backend engineering allows Refokus to accept complex, higher-contract-value client requirements without hiring full-time backend engineers or managing infrastructure devops.
- **Entry Commercial Model:** Project-Based Wholesale Development on a fixed-scope milestone deliverable (e.g., building a Supabase-backed API integration for a Refokus enterprise client).
